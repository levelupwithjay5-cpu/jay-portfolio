# Jay Lord Dela Cruz

**Operations Architect | Customer Success Leader | CRM & Automation Specialist**

## Hero

I build the systems that let growing businesses run without breaking. Ten years across technical support, operations leadership, and CRM architecture — now focused on automation and AI that turn messy workflows into ones that run themselves.

## The short version

I didn't start in marketing or automation. I started fixing computers at an internet café, then spent a decade in technical support, escalation management, and operations leadership before automation and AI became the tools I used to solve the same problem I'd always solved: businesses drowning in manual work.

I've been the first point of contact during a VPN outage. I've built the process that took a support team from reactive to Lean Six Sigma–certified. I've automated an entire webinar business end to end. I've migrated legal firms off DRIP, rebuilt CRM stacks for real estate platforms, and stood up AI voice and conversational systems for companies that needed to scale support without scaling headcount.

The thread through all of it: find where the process breaks, fix it, and build something that doesn't need to be fixed again.

## What I've built

- **Took a support team to Lean Six Sigma Green Belt–certified process discipline**, as SME Process Lead for a VPN software company — redesigning internal workflows and becoming the primary CRM administrator (Zendesk, SnapEngage) for the whole support org.
- **Built and led a technical support department from the ground up** at an early-stage fintech startup — promoted to department lead within three months, then to technical support lead, becoming the bridge between customers, operations, and engineering.
- **Fully automated a webinar company's operations** using Make.com — registration, reminders, attendance confirmation, and certificate generation, with zero manual steps.
- **Ran agency-wide GoHighLevel operations** as Administrator for a white-labeled coaching organization — onboarding, snapshot management, automation deployment, and daily technical coaching for the client community.
- **Delivered CRM and AI migrations across legal, real estate, medical, and coaching industries** — including a full DRIP-to-GoHighLevel migration with membership and webhook automation, and voice AI / conversational AI systems for a real estate CRM provider.

## How I work

Systems first, tools second. I don't lead with the platform — I lead with what's broken and what it costs the business, then build the fix using whatever CRM, automation, or AI stack fits (GoHighLevel, Zendesk, HubSpot, Make.com, Zapier, Retool, and more). Certified GoHighLevel Administrator, Lean Six Sigma Green Belt trained.
