# Design tokens — adapted from BackendOperators brand guide

Source: `C:\Users\Jay\Downloads\BackendOperators-Brand-Guide.dc.html` (internal reference, not public). Used here as a **design system reference only** — palette, type, and voice rules — for Jay's personal portfolio. Not the BackendOperators wordmark, logo, or business copy.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#FAFAF8` | Page background |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, surfaces |
| `--surface-alt` | `#F6FAF9` | Secondary/off-white surface |
| `--ink` | `#111827` | Primary text, dark sections |
| `--muted` | `#6B7280` | Secondary text |
| `--border` | `#E5E7EB` | Borders, dividers |
| `--accent` | `#14B8A6` | CTAs, links, emphasis — use sparingly (~10%) |
| `--accent-deep` | `#0F9488` | Accent gradients/depth |

Ratio: ~60% neutral base, ~30% supporting gray, ~10% accent teal. Accent impact depends on restraint — don't overuse.

## Type

- **Headings (H1–H3):** Manrope, weight 700–800, sentence case, letter-spacing ~-0.02em at large sizes.
- **Body/UI:** Inter, weight 400 regular / 500 medium for emphasis. Avoid bold body text.
- Fallback stack: `-apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
- Google Fonts: `Manrope:wght@500;600;700;800` and `Inter:wght@400;500;600`

## Voice (adapted for a personal portfolio)

- **Outcomes before mechanism** — lead with what was built/fixed/improved, save tool names (GHL, Zendesk, Make.com, etc.) for supporting detail.
- **No hedging** — no "might help," "could potentially." State it plainly.
- **No hype words** — no "revolutionary," "game-changing." Specificity does the convincing.
- Short, direct sentences. Confident, not salesy.
- Unlike BackendOperators (which uses "we" even solo), this is a personal site — use **"I."**

## Visual style

- Generous whitespace, one confident accent color (teal), soft shadows.
- Quiet depth only: subtle shadow or restrained teal glow under cards/CTAs — never neon, bevels, or skeuomorphic effects.
- Photography: real photo of Jay + real screenshots/results over stock or illustration.
- Avoid: glowing circuit boards, robot imagery, purple-blue gradients.

## Not carried over

- BackendOperators name, logo mark (`{ }` bracket), and tagline — this is Jay's personal brand, not the BackendOperators business.
- "We" voice, founder-secondary positioning — this site is founder-primary (it's a personal portfolio).
